About Cycle log

A private daily log for mood, symptoms and periods. No account, and nothing leaves this phone.

Today
Period, mood and “any symptoms?” If yes, pick the kind and rate it. Low-effort day? Answer just mood, or Skip today. Swipe to change days.
History
Cycles, Year or Timeline, colored by mood or symptoms. Tap a day to open it.
Periods
Mark past periods on a calendar or paste them from a spreadsheet.

Rating scale

Focus mode keeps the days a doctor’s report compares and roughly halves the symptom days. The full 1–6 scale matches the clinical DRSP form and can be scored with C-PASS. Pick a scale and stick with it: days rated on different scales can only be compared approximately.

Where your entries live

The website only delivers this page. Everything you enter is saved in this browser on this device and is never sent to the site or anyone else.

Install this page as an app and always open it from that icon. It works without an internet connection. Clearing your browser’s data for this site, removing the app, or switching browsers can erase entries. Export a backup regularly.

Export

One row per logged day, with columns date, cycle_day (day 1 = first bleeding day), days_to_next_period (−1 = the day before), menses, mood (1–5), scale (0-3 or 1-6), skipped, item01–item24 in DRSP order, and notes. Ratings are stored on the scale shown in the scale column.

Import a backup

Choose a CSV exported from this page, or paste its contents below. Imported days replace any days with the same date.
