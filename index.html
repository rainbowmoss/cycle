Where your entries live

The website only delivers this page. Everything you enter is saved in this browser on this device and is never sent to the site or anyone else.

Install this page as an app and always open it from that icon. It works without an internet connection. Clearing Chrome’s data for this site, uninstalling the app, or switching browsers can erase entries. Export a backup regularly.

Export

One row per logged day, with columns date, cycle_day (day 1 = first bleeding day), days_to_next_period (−1 = the day before), menses, item01–item24 in DRSP order, and notes. Ratings are on a 0–3 scale (0 = not at all, 3 = severe).
