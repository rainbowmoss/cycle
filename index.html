Questions

Symptom questions
Rating scale

Focus mode keeps the days a doctor’s report compares and roughly halves the symptom days. The full 1–6 scale matches the clinical DRSP form and can be scored with C-PASS. Pick a scale and stick with it: days rated on different scales can only be compared approximately.

My trackers

Add anything else you want to rate each day, like “Felt flat” or “Restless”. Each is rated None, Mild, Moderate or Severe.

Medications

Daily and as-needed meds appear on Today so you can tick them off. Long-acting ones (like an IUD) are noted there and in your summary. Use a nickname if you’d rather the name not show on screen. The app can’t send reminders, so a phone alarm still helps.

When do you take it?

It shows up that many days before your period is due, and stays until your period starts.

About and how to edit

Cycle log is a private daily log for period, mood, symptoms, movement and meds. Everything stays in this browser on this device.

To change the app yourself: the whole app is the index.html file in your GitHub repo. On GitHub, open the file, tap the pencil icon, make your edit, and tap Commit changes. Then open sw.js the same way and raise the version number in cycle-log-v… by one, so phones load the update. Open the app twice to see it.

You can also paste index.html into Claude and describe the change you want. The repo’s README has more detail, including how friends can copy it with Fork.

Symptom items are adapted from the Daily Record of Severity of Problems (Endicott, Nee & Harrison, 2006). This is a personal log, not a diagnosis.

Summary report

A summary of your recent cycles: mood and symptom patterns, the week before vs. after your period, cycle lengths and meds. Print it, save it as a PDF, or share it with your doctor.

Backup and move devices

An encrypted backup is one file locked with a passphrase you choose. Keep it somewhere like Google Drive or iCloud, then restore it on your phone or computer. Without the passphrase nobody can open it, including you, so write it down somewhere safe.

Plain CSV export or import

Export CSV

One row per logged day, with columns date, cycle_day (day 1 = first bleeding day), days_to_next_period (−1 = the day before), menses, mood (1–5), scale (0-3 or 1-6), skipped, item01–item24 in DRSP order, notes, movement, and one med: column per medication. Ratings are stored on the scale shown in the scale column.

Import CSV

Choose a CSV exported from this page, or paste its contents below. Imported days replace any days with the same date.

Add past periods

Mark periods from before you started using the app, on a calendar or by pasting dates from a spreadsheet.

Import from another app

Apple Health (iPhone): in the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Export All Health Data. Unzip the file and choose export.xml below. Only period and spotting days are read.

This hasn’t been tested with every iOS version yet. You can undo it under Add past periods.

Other apps (Clue, Flo and others): export or request your data from the app, open it in a spreadsheet, then use the paste option in Add past periods.

Where your entries live

The website only delivers this page. Everything you enter is saved in this browser on this device and is never sent to the site or anyone else.

Always open the app from its icon. Clearing your browser’s data for this site, removing the app, or switching browsers can erase entries, so back up regularly.
